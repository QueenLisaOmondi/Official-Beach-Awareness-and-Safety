Rip Current Safety

Your quick guide to spotting danger and escaping safely.

If caught in a rip current:

  • • Stay calm and conserve your energy
  • • Don't fight it – you can't swim straight in
  • • Swim parallel to the shoreline to escape
  • • Float and call for help if you can't break free

Respect Marine Life

Be a guardian of the blue.

Protect ocean ecosystems:

  • • Look, don't touch: Coral is alive and fragile
  • • Choose reusables: Refuse single-use plastics
  • • Wear reef-safe sunscreen to prevent chemical damage
  • • Leave no trace – take your trash and beyond
Learn More

Sun Protection

Enjoy the sun safely.

Sun safety essentials:

  • • Reapply SPF 30+ every 2 hours & after swimming
  • • Seek shade during peak intensity (10am-4pm)
  • • Cover up with a hat, sunglasses, and a shirt
  • • Drink water consistently to avoid heat exhaustion
