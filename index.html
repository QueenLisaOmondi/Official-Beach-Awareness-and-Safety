Essential Beach Safety

Rip Current Safety

Know the signs, save a life

Rip Current Facts

Rip currents cause 80% of beach rescues. Look for channels of churning, discolored water moving away from shore. If caught: don't panic, swim parallel to shore, then back to beach.

80% of rescues involve rip currents

Sun Protection

Shield yourself from UV damage

UV Protection Guide

Use SPF 30+ sunscreen, reapply every 2 hours. Wear UV-protective clothing and sunglasses. Seek shade during peak hours (10 AM - 4 PM).

1 in 5 Americans develop skin cancer

Respect Marine Life

Observe, don't disturb

Wildlife Guidelines

Keep 150 feet from marine mammals. Never touch or feed wildlife. If you see stranded animals, contact local authorities immediately. Your actions protect entire ecosystems.

100K+ marine animals die from human interference yearly

Eco Behavior

Leave only footprints

Environmental Impact

Pack out all trash, use reef-safe sunscreen, avoid disturbing dunes and vegetation. Small actions create massive positive change for ocean health.

73% of beach litter is plastic

How You Can Help

Join Beach Cleanups

Participate in local cleanup events or organize your own with friends and classmates.

Use Reef-Safe Sunscreen

Choose mineral sunscreens without oxybenzone and octinoxate to protect coral reefs.

Minimize Waste

Bring reusable water bottles, bags, and containers to reduce single-use plastic consumption.

Spread Awareness

Share knowledge with friends, family, and social media to multiply your positive impact.

Stay Educated

Follow NOAA, Oceana, and National Geographic for the latest ocean conservation research.

Support Ocean Policy

Contact representatives about marine protection legislation and sustainable practices.
